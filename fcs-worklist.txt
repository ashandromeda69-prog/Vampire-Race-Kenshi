1. RE-WIRE RECRUIT HIRING (the one functional gap; ~2 minutes)
   The custom hire line "Ok, you're hired." [4021] lost its effects -
   they lived in Dialogue.mod records. In v3.5 they were:
      conditions: [51951-Dialogue.mod, value 5000]
      effects:    [5679-Recruits Dialogue.mod, 0] + [51948-Dialogue.mod, 5000]
   i.e. require 5000 cats, join squad, deduct 5000. In FCS, open the line
   and add the vanilla equivalents: condition CO_PLAYER_MONEY >= 5000,
   effects DA_JOINS_SQUAD_FAST + money deduction, mirroring any vanilla
   bar-recruit line. Until then the recruits talk but cannot be hired.

3. SPECIAL-FOOD GAP (design decision)
   v3.5 makes Bread [1946], Rice Bowl [1016] and Potatoes [1960] 'special
   food' (item function 15) and grants eating rights per race. Vanilla
   Greenlander/Shek/Hive Prince edits are missing (Ian's other mods covered
   them), so on a clean install those races cannot eat these three foods.
   Either add 'special food' entries for them in FCS, or revert the three
   item-function edits to disable the gate.

4. SUN-BURN SYSTEM (quarantined in v3.6.1)
   v3.5 set acid 1.0 / weather strength 10 on biome records 18001/18002/
   18003 and weather protection on 58 armours. Those biome IDs' vanilla
   identity could not be verified (Ian's world was renamed by rebirth.mod),
   and only 8 animal races were made immune - vanilla humans would burn.
   Rebuild in FCS against verified vanilla biomes with proper immunities.
